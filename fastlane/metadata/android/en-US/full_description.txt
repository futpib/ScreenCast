ScreenCast mirrors your Android screen, plays media on a TV, and controls Android TV or Google TV from one app. It does not require Google Home, Google Play Services, or a Google account.

Choose what you want to do on the home screen:

* Mirror my screen: pick one TV. The app tries stock Cast Streaming, then its WebRTC receiver, then HLS if a connection fails. Android asks for screen-sharing consent on each attempt.
* Play a file or link: choose a photo, video, or audio file, or enter a direct media URL. TVs offering both Cast and DLNA appear once; the app can retry through DLNA if Cast fails.
* Control my TV: pair with an Android TV or Google TV for remote control.
* Mirror to several TVs: share one HLS capture with up to four Cast receivers.

Advanced users can select Native Cast Streaming, WebRTC, HLS, Media, or Remote directly and adjust their technical settings. Casting runs in a foreground service with a Stop action. Playback audio capture is available on Android 10 and newer, subject to Android's capture rules.

Requires Android 8.0 or newer and compatible devices on the same local network. Receiver support and media formats vary by TV.
